# Rights Gate and Platform Safety

This is not legal advice. It's the operating rules that keep the channel alive.

## 1. Own footage first
Rockstar has historically allowed creators to post and monetize gameplay video they capture themselves. Check the current policy on rockstargames.com before monetizing, because it can change. **Your own capture is your safest, highest-value asset.** Every archetype marked "own gameplay" assumes you bought and recorded the game.

## 2. Other creators' footage
Streamer and YouTuber VODs are **their** copyrighted work. Re-uploading or lightly trimming them leads to Content ID claims, strikes and bad blood in a small creator world.
If you use excerpts:
- Keep each one short, and cut away to your commentary often.
- **Credit on screen and in the description** (name, platform, link).
- Add a real transformation: ranking, analysis, a counterpoint, a test, or a joke the source didn't make.
- Prefer creators who publicly allow clipping or run clip programs. Many large streamers do. Check their pinned rules. "Clipping gta 6" is a rising keyword (up 340%), and some streamers pay clippers.
- If you get a takedown, respect it. Don't re-upload.

## 3. Hard NO
- Leaked builds, stolen pre-release footage, or datamined assets shared before release. Rockstar and Take-Two enforce these aggressively.
- Pirated copies or early-unlock exploits (one streamer was warned of a Twitch suspension over an unlock-time trick at launch).
- Movie or TV clips in "NPC reaction" edits, unless they're licensed. Replace them with your own gameplay.

## 4. Platform content flags (launch-week reality)
- YouTube sent warnings to several creators over **in-game nudity** within hours of launch. Blur it or avoid it, especially in thumbnails and the first 3 seconds.
- Keep extreme violence out of thumbnails. Age restriction kills Shorts reach.
- Use Kick as a backup platform for live coverage only. Distribute clips on YouTube, TikTok and Reels.

## 5. Gate checklist (all must be YES)
1. Main footage is ours, licensed, or generated.
2. Any third-party excerpt is short, credited, and commented on.
3. The clip adds an idea the source lacked.
4. No leaked, pirated, or exploit material.
5. Thumbnail and first 3 seconds are platform-safe.
