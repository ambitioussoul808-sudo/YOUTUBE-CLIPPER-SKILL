# Source Map: the 4 Tiers

Streamer clips alone make you late and borrowed. Scan all four tiers, and use Tier 4 to decide which of Tiers 1–3 to act on.

| Tier | What | Where | Tooling | Best clip angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Official** | Trailers, Newswire posts, patch notes, GTA Online updates, Take-Two earnings, ratings | rockstargames.com/newswire, @RockstarGames (X/IG/TikTok/YouTube), take2games.com investor news | WebSearch / web-fetch | "Everything you missed", frame-by-frame breakdowns, "what this means for…" |
| **2. Creators** | Streamer reactions, walkthroughs, stunts, reviews | YouTube, Twitch, Kick, IG/TikTok creator accounts | `vidiq_trending_videos`, `vidiq_outliers`, `vidiq_youtube_search`, StreamsCharts / TwitchTracker (web) | Reaction rankings, "I tried what X did", recreate-and-beat, counter-takes |
| **3. Community** | Memes, POV skits, theories, outrage, polls | TikTok, Reels, Shorts, r/GTA6, r/GamingLeaksAndRumours (rumors only, never leaks), X | `vidiq_instagram_tiktok_outlier_search`, WebSearch | POV/skit formats, "the internet is losing it over…", theory videos |
| **4. Demand** | What people are *searching* for | YouTube search | `vidiq_keyword_research` (rising, questions, matching_terms) | Titles, tags, and which topics deserve a video at all |

## Cadence
| Phase | Scan frequency | Rising period |
|---|---|---|
| Launch week / trailer week | every 6–12h | `day` |
| First month post-launch | daily | `week` |
| Steady state | 2× weekly | `week` / `month` |

## Signals that start a wave (watch for these)
- New Rockstar Newswire post or trailer drop: Tier 1 leads by 2–6h
- Region unlock times (NZ first): the first clips appear here
- A "first-ever" moment (first stolen plane, first 5-star escape, first glitch)
- A platform enforcement story (YouTube nudity warnings, Twitch bans): debate content
- Review scores and Metacritic milestones
- Sales and streaming records: "biggest launch ever" framing
