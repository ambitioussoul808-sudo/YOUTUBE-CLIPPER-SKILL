# Intentional Empire: Brand Kit for Pinterest

## Identity in one line
Spiritual rebellion meets spreadsheet. **Defiant healing over toxic positivity.** Sovereign, cosmic, elevated, and practical enough to act on today.

## Audience
Ambitious women and builders (roughly 28–45) who are rebuilding: after burnout, grief, financial chaos, or outgrowing an old identity. They want **economic sovereignty** *and* inner peace, and they're allergic to hustle-bro and "good vibes only" content.

## Content pillars (map every pin to one)
| # | Pillar | Pinterest search territory |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Economic Sovereignty** | money mindset, budgeting for women, emergency fund, financial literacy, generational wealth, income streams |
| 2 | **Defiant Healing** | grief healing, nervous system regulation, burnout recovery, shadow work, Grief vs. UnRest Framework™ |
| 3 | **Identity Evolution** | becoming her, 2027 vision board, self-concept, numerology / Life Path, Aquarius energy, glow-up (inner) |
| 4 | **Empire Systems** | content systems, chatbot/automation, funnels, Notion templates, CEO routines, solopreneur workflows |
| 5 | **Sovereign Motherhood & Legacy** | mom entrepreneur, family finance, legacy planning, intentional living |

## Voice rules
- **Do:** bold, high-contrast statements; humor with a raised eyebrow; permission-giving; name the feeling before giving the fix; modular steps.
- **Don't:** toxic positivity ("just stay positive!"), shame, fake urgency, income or return guarantees, therapy claims.
- **Signature moves:** "Not ___. ___." reframes · "Your nervous system isn't broken, it's ___." · numbered sovereign steps · the crown ✦ as a divider.
- **Words we own:** sovereign, empire, intentional, defiant, unrest, evolve, crown, ecosystem, permission.

## Visual system
- **Palette:** Blue ✦ Green ✦ Gold (hex values in `../brand.json`).
  - **Sovereign Blue**: authority, calm, trust. Default for money and systems pins.
  - **Growth Green**: wealth, renewal, healing. Default for healing and growth pins.
  - **Crown Gold**: highlights, keywords, dividers, the ✦ mark. **10% of the canvas or less.** Never body text on ivory (low contrast).
  - **Ivory**: breathing room and a premium feel. Default for checklists and infographics.
  - **Legacy Burgundy**: optional accent for EmPress / high-feminine pieces only.
- **Typography:** serif headline (editorial authority) + clean sans body. One emphasized keyword per pin, set in gold.
- **Composition:** 2:3 vertical (1000×1500). Headline readable at thumbnail size, 7 words or fewer when possible. Branded footer with the handle or URL.
- **Imagery:** warm, real, textured (desk flatlays, journaling hands, soft light, Black and brown women represented). No stock-smile clichés.
- **Contrast:** text/background contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Check it when swapping hex values.
