# Content Guardrails (Reach-Safe, Disclaimer-Light)

Disclaimers on a pin read as hesitation and cost saves. What actually protects reach is avoiding the claims Pinterest limits or removes: guaranteed money results and health or medical claims. So the copy carries the credibility, and disclaimers are the exception.

## Never (these protect distribution and trust)
- Promise or imply returns, income amounts, or "guaranteed" / "get rich fast" results. Pinterest restricts this kind of content, and it breaks the brand's anti-hype stance.
- Present one financial product as right for everyone.
- Use another company's name, logo or product names without permission.
- Show income or results testimonials as typical outcomes.
- Make medical or therapeutic claims ("cures anxiety", "heals trauma", "replaces therapy").

## Write it safe, not hedged
Make the framing itself the safety net, so the pin never needs a hedge:
| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Make $5K/month with this" | "The 4 income streams I'd build first" |
| "This will fix your money" | "The money questions nobody asked you" |
| "Heal your trauma in 7 days" | "7 prompts for the grief you haven't named" |
| "Invest in X now" | "What to understand before you invest" |

Sovereign POV ("what I do", "what to ask", "what to notice") is more viral *and* needs no disclaimer.

## Disclaimers: when (rarely) to use them
- **Never on the pin image itself.**
- **Mindset, healing reflection, identity, systems, budgeting basics:** no disclaimer.
- **Only** when a pin gives specific investing, debt-payoff or tax steps, or names financial products: add one short line at the *end* of the description:
  *"Education, not personal financial advice."*
- **Only** when healing content touches crisis-level topics (suicidal thoughts, abuse, severe depression): add at the end of the description:
  *"Support matters. Please reach out to a professional if you're struggling."*

## Copyright and originality
- Remix patterns only. No reused images, text, characters, logos or watermarks from source pins.
- Use your own photos, licensed stock, or generated imagery.
- Never claim another creator's framework as yours. Your own frameworks (e.g. Grief vs. UnRest Framework™) are your differentiator.

## Quick check (attach to every pin)
`[ ] No guarantees  [ ] No medical claims  [ ] Safe-by-framing copy  [ ] Disclaimer only if specific advice  [ ] No third-party marks  [ ] Original`
