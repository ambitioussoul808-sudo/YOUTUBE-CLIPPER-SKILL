# Genspark Clip Genius Prompts: Launch Day (2026-11-19)

Clip Genius (genspark.ai/clip_genius) edits a video from **one prompt**. You give it a video (upload or URL) and it analyzes the footage, picks moments, cuts, adds captions, transitions and music sync. These prompts turn each clip brief variant into an edit order.

## How to use
1. **Record first.** Clip Genius edits footage. It doesn't play the game for you.
2. **Upload your own recordings** (PS5/Xbox capture, phone video). Only paste a URL if it's *your own* video or the creator allows clipping (see Brief 2).
3. Paste the prompt for that variant and fill in the `[brackets]`.
4. Download it and check it against the Rights Gate (bottom of this file) before posting.
5. Reviewers call Clip Genius "hit-or-miss", so the prompts are deliberately precise. If the first cut misses, re-run with: "Keep the same structure but use the moment at [timestamp] as the hook."

Save what you made, and any timestamps Clip Genius picked, in `.claude/skills/gta6-viral-clipper/data/genspark-2026-11-19.md`.

## Brief 1: "World's First ___ in GTA 6"
**Upload:** your raw gameplay recording of the 5-star chase (or whichever first you got).

### 1A · Short (35–45s, vertical)
```
Edit this GTA 6 gameplay into a 35–45 second vertical (9:16) YouTube Short / TikTok.

Structure:
- 0–2s: open on the single most intense moment (explosion, crash, or last-second escape). Big on-screen text: "WORLD'S FIRST [5-STAR ESCAPE] IN GTA 6" in bold white with a black outline, top third.
- 2–6s: rewind effect back to the calm start. Text: "how it started 👇"
- 6–35s: show the escalation in 4–6 fast cuts, one every 3–5 seconds, each adding a bigger threat (police → helicopter → roadblock → SWAT). Add a 1–5 star counter graphic that fills up.
- 35–42s: the payoff (escape or spectacular fail). Freeze-frame on the peak with a bass-drop hit.
- End on a frame that matches the opening so the video loops.

Audio: high-energy phonk / bass-boosted beat with the drop synced to the payoff. Keep game audio underneath, quieter. Auto-captions on, bold, center-bottom.
Crop to keep the action centered in vertical. Blur any nudity.
```
