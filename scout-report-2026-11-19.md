## Top 3 moves today
1. **Run a "first-ever" chaos video within 24 hours.** Stolen planes and jets are the breakout gameplay moment. Typical Gamer's "World's First Stolen Plane" got 1.03M views in under a day. GameV's jumbo jet video beat a 320K-sub channel 2.3×. Find a *different* first: first 5-star escape, first alligator encounter, first boat heist. Title format: "I ___ in GTA 6… then ___".
2. **Make a streamer reaction roundup with real commentary.** This had the highest score and highest engagement in the scan: Asmongold Clips, 411K views/hour, 8.5% engagement. Rank the top 10 launch reactions (Kai Cenat, IShowSpeed, HoneyMad) with your verdict on each. "kai cenat gta 6" searches are up 570%, and "ishowspeed gta 6" has 470K/mo searches. Credit every clip and keep each excerpt short.
3. **Ride "GTA 6 map" and "GTA 6 vs GTA 5" with your own capture.** Combined, these get more than 1M searches a month and are growing 129–149%. A small channel (GameV) won both formats. Competition scores are mid (63–66), so there's still room.

**Bonus, zero-gameplay Short (post today):** a "POV: ___ when GTA 6 drops" life skit. These hit 1M+ views even on tiny accounts. One had 842× the creator's median views.
