## Production notes
- Several source clips were only available at 360p and were upscaled, so they're soft on big screens. For final posts, download the HD/4K versions from the Pexels pages above.
- Swap the generated audio for a **trending sound in the TikTok/Reels/Shorts audio library** when you post. That's licensed for the platform and boosts reach.
- Best use: post these as "bridge" content while your own gameplay capture renders, then stitch your real gameplay into the same template (replace shots 4–6).
